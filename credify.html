Digitizing trade for Ugandan importers

Simplifying cross border trade, shipment tracking and credit access for businesses navigating the complex corridor between East Africa and China.

Credify contracted me for a brief period in 2025 to design their online solution.

USER INTERVIEWS

Participants expressed difficulty in understanding Import Requirements & Regulations.

Navigating import licenses, the ‘negative list’, and taxes is not easy for the average importer.

Customers rely on various 3rd party tools for visibility of cargo, financing

Importers often rely on third-party agents and don't have visibility of cargo once it lands at Mombasa Port.

Many importers borrow from informal lenders at high interest rates due to lack of access to invoice-based financing.

Payments are typically done through multiple apps and channels, adding complexity and friction.

THE SOLUTION

Based on the learnings, we aimed to simplify and increase visibility

Understanding the logistical and financial complexity faced by Ugandans importing goods—such as inland tracking difficulties, evolving fuel import policies, and rising import costs—shaped our product strategy for Credify.

We prioritized clarity, accessibility, and automation to replace opaque, offline systems with intuitive digital experiences.

The app simplifies key pain points like shipment tracking, wallet interest, and loan eligibility with clear feedback and real-time status updates, empowering users navigating volatile forex rates and unpredictable last-mile delivery.

Scope of MVP

  • Authentication: Secure login with wallet PIN
  • Dashboard: Overview of loans, wallet balance, active shipments
  • Shipping: Create shipment requests, view quotations, track shipments
  • Payments: Manage invoice payments and shipping fees
  • Loans: Request loans, see eligibility tiers, repayment status
  • Wallet: Top-up, withdraw, interest view, statements
  • Shipping Tracking: Visibility from port to delivery—with known gaps in inland tracking addressed through UI cues

Prioritized clarity by using supporting copy that expounds the meaning of the term in a language users understand

Data-heavy screens (e.g., wallet interest, shipping quotes) were designed to be digestible on mobile

Replacing skepticism with trust by providing real time updates and shipment tracking

Other screens

While still in pilot, Credify now has a functional MVP with:

  • A simplified, mobile-first interface
  • Clear flows for top-ups, loan eligibility, and shipment tracking
  • Interfaces designed to support users with limited digital or logistical literacy
