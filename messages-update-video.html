ServCraft · Messages Update

A Brief on What Changed

A two-minute walkthrough of the messages redesign, before the full case study.

What I've come to appreciate, working on a product like ServCraft, is how much people rely on it. They're running their businesses through it, keeping staff aligned in crucial moments, staying up to date on outstanding payments, and responding to customers quickly and efficiently, simplifying work and job admin for busy trade people.

The awareness that thousands of people are using this throughout their work day prompted us to ask: how might we make the messages experience intuitive for new users without making it too unfamiliar for existing users?

This video captures the changes at a glance. The primary approach was Hick's law: reducing options to reduce the time taken or effort required.

When the messages redesign shipped two months ago, what I was watching closest was how well people would take to the change. It landed just as we hoped. It was picked up without a hitch.

Proud of what we shipped, and proud to be part of a product that keeps getting better alongside the businesses that rely on it.
