Product designer turning complex systems into interfaces people actually enjoy using.

I'm Tyra, a product designer with 6 years of experience across fintech, field service software, and B2B SaaS. I've shipped work used by thousands of people across South Africa, East Africa, and beyond, from enterprise scheduling tools to mobile trade finance apps.

I treat AI as a real design partner. It lets me move faster, take on more scope, and explore interaction models that used to be out of reach. Lately I'm drawn to agentic workflows, voice interfaces, and the design questions that show up when software starts acting on your behalf.

Good design has to survive contact with production code. I work close to engineering, care about the details that make a system usable at scale, and build for the messy version of the problem, not the simplified one.
